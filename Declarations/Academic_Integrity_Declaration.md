### Declaration of Originality

I hereby declare that the capstone project titled **"Automotive Cybersecurity Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment (TARA) Assistant Using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)"** submitted by me is an authentic record of my own work carried out under the guidance of my faculty advisor and industrial mentors.

---

### Key Declarations & Academic Compliance

1. **AI Development Assistance:** AI development tools and AI models were utilized during the design, coding, testing, and documentation phases as declared in detail within the accompanying `AI_Tool_Usage_Declaration.md`. Code, mathematical formulas, evaluation benchmarks, and documentation generated with AI assistance were inspected, understood, and tested using the automated test suite.

2. **Open-Source Libraries & Frameworks:** This project incorporates standard open-source Python libraries (FastAPI, Streamlit, ChromaDB, Sentence-Transformers, PyMuPDF, Plotly, Graphviz, ReportLab, pytest) as specified in `requirements.txt`. All libraries are used in compliance with their respective open-source licenses.

3. **Pre-trained Embeddings:** The project utilizes the pre-trained dense embedding model `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` (via sentence-transformers) for semantic text vector representation. The embedding model is used strictly as a static feature extractor for inference and vector similarity search.

4. **Synthetic Knowledge Base & Privacy:** All vehicle specification documents, ECU inventories, message matrices, threat scenarios, and mitigation controls contained within `Input_Data/` consist of synthetic sample data created exclusively for educational and academic research purposes. No proprietary, confidential, trade-secret, or real-world OEM automotive documents were used.

5. **Industry Standards Reference:** References to industry engineering standards (**ISO/SAE 21434:2021** and **UNECE Regulation No. 155**) are cited solely for contextual grounding and architectural alignment. This academic prototype does not claim formal certification, regulatory approval, or autonomous compliance status.

6. **Advisory AI Outputs & Human Review:** All AI-assisted TARA findings—including asset security properties, threat scenarios, risk ratings, defensive mitigations, and candidate cybersecurity requirements—are candidate suggestions intended solely to assist human engineers. Every output requires qualified human cybersecurity review and validation.
